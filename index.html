Your resume profile

How to get your LinkedIn PDF
  1. Open your LinkedIn profile on a computer (Me → View Profile).
  2. Click Resources (or More) under your name → Save to PDF.
  3. Upload that file here. It is read inside your browser and never uploaded anywhere.
  4. Use "Save profile file" to keep a backup or move your profile to another device/browser.

Review and edit

LinkedIn PDFs don't include projects and often lack bullet points, so add them here; they make the biggest difference to tailored resumes. Formats are shown in each box.
